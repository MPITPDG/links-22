What each mark on this dashboard means, and where it comes from
Service type (Margin tab)
Freight Forwarding (FFD)Freight / transport leg only.
Custom Clearance (CCLR)Customs clearance leg only.
Exwork (EX)Ex-works / door-to-door — both legs included.
FFD + CCLRBoth freight forwarding and clearance done on the job.
Misc / OtherNon-shipment (misc) jobs — billed value but no job-wise P&L source.
UnclassifiedNo service information anywhere — shown as its own row rather than guessed.
Where the service type came from
JOB Ticked on the import job itself (Ex-works / Custom Clearance / Freight Forwarding) — recorded by the operator, most reliable.
no badge From the shipment-summary flags (FFD / CCLR / EXW / FFDCLR) — the same source the Management Dashboard uses.
INFERRED Nothing recorded (mainly sea exports since Apr-2024, when the flag writer stopped). Worked out from the job's own invoice: Agency Charges head → clearance, Origin THC head → forwarding, both → FFD + CCLR. Validated at ~96% accuracy on 11,451 flagged sea-export jobs. Applied to sea export only. The money is not affected — only the bucket is inferred. On a client row, INFERRED 21/31 = 21 of 31 jobs inferred.
Client risk (Receivables tab)
REDAct now: risk score ≥ 60, or over half the balance is older than a year, or the client is marked DEFAULT / COURT CASE in the master.
AMBERWatch: risk score 30–59, or half the balance is older than 180 days.
GREENFine: within the suggested limit, paying roughly on time, little old balance.
NO-TERMSNo credit period on file for this client — measured against a 30-day default, so "late" is an assumption. Never shown GREEN.
SETTLEDNothing outstanding and nothing unbilled.
