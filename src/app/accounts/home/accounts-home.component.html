How to read this page

every figure in plain words. The icon on any card opens the same note.
Month to dateFrom the 1st of this month up to today. "Last month" means the same number of days in the previous month, so the comparison is fair.
InvoicedTotal of tax invoices raised in the period, GST included. Proforma invoices are not counted.
CollectedReceipts and journal adjustments applied against invoices in the period. Money received but not yet matched to a bill is not here.
OutstandingWhat all clients still owe today, after receipts and credit notes already applied. A balance, not a monthly figure.
OverdueThe part of Outstanding that has crossed the client's credit period. Follow these up first.
AgeingOutstanding split by days since the invoice date. Green is fresh; red has been unpaid for more than 180 days.
Jobs openedShipments registered in the period, by mode. Billed means at least one tax invoice exists on the job; Unbilled means none yet.
GOPGross operating profit: billed value minus purchase cost on jobs opened in the period. Costs are often booked before the sale invoice, so early in the month GOP can look low or negative.
MarginGOP as a percentage of revenue. 20% means 20 rupees kept from every 100 rupees billed.
Cash positionWhat clients owe us minus what we owe suppliers, both as of today. Supplier bills are read for the last 12 months; older unpaid ones are treated as dead data.
Cash forecastOpen amounts on both sides grouped by when they fall due, net of unapplied receipts and supplier advances. "Already overdue" is due now and equals the Overdue card. Where a supplier bill has no credible due date, 30 days from the bill date is assumed.
Days to collect / payHow long invoices and supplier bills of the last 90 days took to settle, weighted by value. If we pay faster than we collect, the gap is funded from our own cash.
Clients to ask aboutOverdue clients ranked by money unpaid for over 180 days. "Pays in" is their actual settlement time against the agreed credit period.
Earned but not collectedInvoices waiting for authorisation (the same list as the Authorisation screen, for this branch), proformas not converted, shipments still unbilled after 15 days, and receipts not matched to a bill. Each line opens the screen that clears it.
Change pillsGreen means better than last month, red means worse. For Overdue and Cost, going up is red.
As ofFigures are computed once and reused for 10 minutes. Refresh recomputes them now.
SourceAll numbers use the same rules as the Finance Dashboard, so the two agree for the same period.
