How this page works

Which jobs (the date used)Export jobs of your branch whose job was created in the system (the entry date stamped when the export job is opened) within the chosen window — last 30 days, 90 days or 1 year, up to today. One per export number; cancelled jobs are left out. Everything else — stage, BL / AWB, ETD — is shown as it stands right now.
Needs attentionA job with no BL / AWB yet whose vessel sails within {{ RISK_DAYS }} days, or sailed in the last {{ LATE_DAYS }} days (in red). Jobs whose vessel sailed longer ago are folded into "older jobs to tidy up" — usually the BL just was not entered.
The week aheadVessels from the export vessel list sailing in the next 7 days, with our jobs by ETD. A tick means the job already has its document.
Where the jobs areEach job sits at the furthest step it has reached: opened, shipping bill, cargo received, carted, containerised (sea), out of charge, document issued, prealert sent.
DocumentedJobs with an FCR / HBL, master BL, MAWB or HAWB number recorded.
Lists and top 5Built from the newest 50 jobs at each step, so they show recent work. The headline numbers always count every job.
